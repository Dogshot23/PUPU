### Notes for review
- Emotional rhythm alternates on purpose (curious → excited → curious → excited → curious → mischievous → mischievous → thoughtful → happy → proud) so no two missions in a row feel the same, per Law 2.
- Missions 4, 6, and 9 give the child two speaking turns (ask + react/share), which fits B1's slightly higher output expectations versus A2.
- Mission 8 is the "occasionally profound" one (Law 5); it's placed mid-batch, not at the start or end, so it doesn't feel like a themed finale.
- All Korean translations are natural renderings of PUPU's voice rather than literal word-for-word translations.
